LANDMARKS OF BELARUS AND ENGLISH SPEAKING COUNTRIES

Mir castle is located in Grondo Region, or to be more exact, in Karelichy district.
The castle was first mentioned in 1527 in the "Lithuanian Metrica"
It was designed as a square from top view with towers exceeding 25 meters in the corners
The width of the walls were 3 meters and their height was 13 meters. After the death of the builder in 1526, the castle's ownership was divided between
his four sons. After the rest died, one son remained. He was married to Sofia Radzivill,
a member of the richest family in the country at that time. And in 1553 his son received
the Graf title. But soon after that, in 1559 he dies and the castle went to the Radzivill family.
They held the ownership for the castle for 3 centuries, but eventually the Polish-Lithuanian
Commonwealth was invaded and the castle was destroyed. There were also fights near the castle
in the French Invasion of Russia. In 1832 the ownership of the Mir castle went to the
Wittgenstein. With the Red Army invading Poland in 1939, the Wittgenstein lost
their control over the castle. And after the second world war reconstructions had begun.

In 1865, Édouard René Lefebvre de Laboulaye came up
with the idea to gift the US a statue, celebrating a century since
the Independence of the US from Britain. The statue had been in work
from 1876 - 1884 and shown on many festivals. On May 21st, 1885,
it was shipped to New York, and after a month it arrived. On October
28th, 1886, the statue had been opened to the public, with the president
of the USA attending the opening ceremony. From 1984 - 1986
the statue was restored. It was also closed on September 11th, 2001
due to the terroristic attacks that hit new york on that day.
